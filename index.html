Keys Please — sandbox

Drop-off: a car with a yellow diamond is waiting at the curb. Its label says why they're here and roughly how long they'll stay. Park quick visits (Gym, Diner) close to the curb and long ones (Steakhouse, Movie) deep in the lot.

Earnings: tip (faster pickup = bigger tip, and steakhouse customers tip best) plus parking at $2 for every minute the car sits parked.

Pickup: a ticket appears. Find that car and send it to the curb. If the curb is full, the valet waits in the lane for a spot. If you pick the wrong car, the keys don't fit.

Queue mode (default): tap a car, then tap where it should go. That adds a numbered order, and the next free valet takes it and drives it there. Keep tapping to line up more. Tap a numbered car to cancel its order.

Other modes, via the button up top: Tap sends a valet but you drag the car; Draw means drag everything.

Shifts: you start with 8 stalls and 1 valet. A shift ends once every customer has come and gone. Then spend your earnings on upgrades and start the next, busier shift. Progress saves on this device (Tune → New career to start over).

Tune lets you change every number and color. Space pauses. Tap to close.
