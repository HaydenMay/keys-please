Pickups

Keys Please — sandbox

Drop-off: a car with a yellow diamond is waiting at the curb. Send a valet to it, then drag from the car (blue ring) to an empty stall.

Pickup: a ticket appears on the right. Find that car, send a valet, then drag it to a free curb spot (the green one is kept free for pickups). Wrong car = the keys don't fit.

Valets: in Draw mode, drag from a valet to a car. In Tap mode, just tap the car. Toggle with the button up top.

Tune lets you change every number and color. Space pauses. Tap to close.
